Mount Fuji is the highest mountain in Japan, standing at 3,776 meters above sea level. It is an active stratovolcano located on Honshu Island, near Tokyo. Known for its beautiful symmetrical cone and snow-covered peak, Mount Fuji is one of Japan's most famous natural landmarks. It holds great cultural and spiritual importance in Japan and has been a subject of many paintings, poems, and photographs. Every year, many visitors and climbers travel to Mount Fuji to enjoy its breathtaking scenery and experience its unique beauty. In 2013, Mount Fuji was recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site for its cultural significance and artistic influence.
